Two SoundTouch 10s can play as a stereo pair: pair them from a speaker's settings, then rename, balance or unpair them from the pair's settings; the pair shows as one speaker, and the app needs Revive ST server 0.0.6 or newer. Setting up internet radio for a paired speaker on another server first asks to unpair it, as the pair wouldn't survive the move.
