App: speakers you've used before show as soon as the app opens, marked "Connecting…" until they answer and "Offline" if they don't (tap to try again, or long-press to forget). The splash stays up until home is ready and fades out, the pull-to-refresh spinner only shows when you pull, and cards appear once their state has loaded. The app reconnects to your speakers when you come back to it. On iPhone, the first launch explains the Local Network permission before iOS asks for it, and screens where no speaker answers say where to turn it on.
