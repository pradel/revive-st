Configuring an unpaired speaker pairs it with the household's own account, shared with the household's other speakers, instead of the shared `revivest-user` one. Preset sync and casting use the speaker's account. The speaker client exposes the speaker's `margeAccountUUID` and `margeURL` in its state.
