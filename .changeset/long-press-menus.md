Long-press menus: press and hold a speaker card to open the player or settings, switch to Bluetooth or AUX, or turn the speaker on or off. Hold a preset to play it or save the current track there. Hold a station to play it on a speaker, add or remove it from favorites, or copy its stream URL. The old speaker detail screen is removed; its preset saving, power and source controls now live in these menus.
