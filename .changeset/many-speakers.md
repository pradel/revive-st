App: easier with several speakers. The mini player, the Home presets and Now Playing control one selected speaker: the one you last opened, played, turned on or played a station on, remembered across launches and never switched because another speaker starts playing. With several speakers the mini player stays at the bottom with a dot per speaker (white for the selected one, grey for others playing, an outline for idle ones), tinted by the artwork of what's playing: swipe it left or right to select another speaker. You can also pick one from the "Change" button on the presets or the speaker's name in Now Playing. Home lists speakers by name, fills the selected speaker's card with its blurred artwork, shows speakers in standby on one line with a power button, keeps offline speakers in their own section, offers "Pause all" when more than one speaker plays, and says when several speakers need internet radio setup. Now Playing shows each preset's artwork and name, and its bottom bar switches the source (Bluetooth, AUX), picks another speaker and opens the speaker's settings. Settings lists your speakers, and an offline one can be tried again or forgotten from there. "Play on…" says whether a speaker is playing, paused or stopped.
