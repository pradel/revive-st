Volume and audio sliders now send their value once, when you let go. AUX and Bluetooth show their source name instead of a blank track line. Long-press menu actions are available to VoiceOver and TalkBack. On Android, settings rows respond to taps anywhere on the row, and the pull-to-refresh spinner is dark. The Logs screen scrolls smoothly with long logs and can be filtered by level. Speaker setup steps are announced to screen readers as they progress, and the scanning animation respects Reduce Motion.
