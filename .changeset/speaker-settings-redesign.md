App: a speaker's settings open on a card with its drawing, model, address and status. The name is edited on its own screen, bass and EQ sliders label the ends of their range and say "Max" or "Min" there, and the presets show as six artwork tiles that play on tap. Internet radio setup moves to an "Advanced" group, next to a new "About this speaker" screen with the speaker's IDs and software versions. Form section titles are small capitals, as elsewhere in the app.
