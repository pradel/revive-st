# Download & Install

Revive ST is available on both iOS and Android devices. You can download the app directly from your device's app store.

## Get the App

<Cards>
  <Card title="Download on the App Store" href="https://apps.apple.com" />
  <Card title="Get it on Google Play" href="https://play.google.com" />
</Cards>

## System Requirements

- **iOS**: iOS 13.0 or later.
- **Android**: Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later.

## What to Expect on First Launch

When you open Revive ST for the first time, the app will guide you through connecting to your speakers:

1. **Location Permissions**: The app will request location permissions. This is strictly required by the OS to scan for local Wi-Fi networks and discover your Bose speakers. We do not track or store your location.
2. **Wi-Fi Connection**: Ensure your device is connected to the same Wi-Fi network that you intend to use for your SoundTouch speakers.
