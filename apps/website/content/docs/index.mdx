Welcome to Revive ST! This guide will help you get your legacy Bose SoundTouch speakers connected and streaming again in under 5 minutes.

Internet radio and presets on SoundTouch speakers come from an online server. When you connect a speaker to your Wi-Fi from the app, it asks which one the speaker should use: **Revive ST**, hosted for you, **your own server** (see [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting)), or the one it already uses.

Already running soundcork, AfterTouch or STR? Your speakers work with the app as they are: it plays radio through the server they already use.
