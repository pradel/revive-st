Revive ST is designed to work seamlessly with **any speaker that used the original Bose SoundTouch app**. If the original app controlled it, Revive ST will too.

## Compatible Models

Below is a breakdown of the core SoundTouch models and their compatibility:

| Model                   | Compatibility   | Known Limitations |
| :---------------------- | :-------------- | :---------------- |
| **SoundTouch 10**       | Fully Supported | None              |
| **SoundTouch 20**       | Fully Supported | None              |
| **SoundTouch 30**       | Fully Supported | None              |
| **SoundTouch Portable** | Fully Supported | None              |

## Stereo pairs

Two SoundTouch 10s can play as one stereo pair: one plays the left channel, the other the right. The app shows the pair as one speaker, under the pair's name.

To make a pair, open the settings of one SoundTouch 10 and tap **Pair with another SoundTouch 10** under **Stereo pair**. Pick the other speaker, choose which one plays left (**Swap left and right**), give the pair a name and tap **Pair**. Both speakers must be on, on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone, set up for Revive ST on the same account, and not in another pair.

Once they're paired, volume, power and source set on either speaker apply to both. To rename the pair or unpair the speakers, open the pair's settings: tap **Name** to rename it, or **Unpair** to make them two speakers again.

The left speaker leads the pair. If the right one drops out, for example because it was unplugged, the left one goes to standby about 30 seconds later. Turn the right speaker back on, or unpair them to play on the left one alone.

Stereo pairs need Revive ST server 0.0.6 or newer. If you [run your own server](/docs/self-hosting), [update it](/docs/self-hosting#update-the-server) first.
